## The definite article

| | singular | plural |
|---|---|---|
| masculine | **el** (l') | **els** |
| feminine | **la** (l') | **les** |

**el** and **la** become **l'** before a vowel or *h*: *l'amic, l'hora, l'illa, l'home*.

Exception: **la** stays **la** before an **unstressed i / u** (and before *hi-, hu-*): *la universitat, la idea, la història, la humitat* — but *l'illa, l'ungla* (stressed vowel).

## Personal article

Before first names Catalan uses an article: **en / la** (Barcelona also **el**): *en Joan, la Maria, el Pere*. Before a vowel: **n' / l'**: *n'Albert, l'Anna, l'Enric*.

## Contractions

Prepositions **a, de, per** fuse with **el / els**:

| | el | els |
|---|---|---|
| a | **al** | **als** |
| de | **del** | **dels** |
| per | **pel** | **pels** |

No contraction when the article is apostrophised: *a l'hotel, de l'avi, per l'autopista* — and never with *la / les*: *a la plaça, de les nenes*.

**ca** (= casa de): *cal metge, cal Joan, ca la Rosa, can Pere* (can = ca + en).

## Apostrophe with de

**de** → **d'** before a vowel or *h*: *d'Olot, d'hora, d'aquí*. Other prepositions never apostrophise: *a Olot, per Anna*.

## Examples

- Vaig **al** mercat i **a l'**escola. Torno **del** cinema i **de l'**oficina.
- **L'**home i **la** dona. **L'**illa i **la** universitat.
- **En** Pere i **l'**Anna viuen **a can** Miquel.
- Un plat **d'**arròs; una tassa **de** cafè.
