Checklist before the B1 exam. Each line links to its guide.

## Verbs

- [Present](/guies/verbs-present): four models, incoatiu *-eix-*, the twelve irregulars.
- [Els tres passats](/guies/verbs-passats): *ahir* → perifràstic, *avui* → indefinit, habit/description → imperfet.
- [Plusquamperfet](/guies/verbs-plusquamperfet): *havia* + participle, "past before past".
- [Futur](/guies/verbs-futur) and [condicional](/guies/verbs-condicional): same irregular stems (*faré / faria, tindré / tindria*).
- [Imperatiu](/guies/verbs-imperatiu): *tu* = 3sg present, *vosaltres* = 2pl present, everything else = subjunctive; negative = *no* + subjunctive.
- [Present de subjuntiu](/guies/verbs-subjuntiu-present): will, need, emotion, doubt, purpose, future time — with a change of subject.
- [Imperfet de subjuntiu](/guies/verbs-subjuntiu-imperfet): *si* + imperfet subj. → condicional; past trigger → imperfet subj.
- [Perífrasis](/guies/perifrasis): *haver de, cal (que), acabar de, tornar a, deixar de, estar + gerund*.
- [Gerundi i participi](/guies/gerundi-participi): no gerund of posteriority; participle agreement with *ser / estar*.

## Pronouns

- [Pronoms febles](/guies/pronoms-febles): *el/la/els/les/ho/en* (CD), *li/els* (CI), *hi* (place), *en* (origin, quantity); before the conjugated verb, after infinitive/gerund/imperative.
- [Combinacions](/guies/pronoms-combinacions): *me'l, te la, se'n, l'hi, la hi, li ho, li'n, n'hi*.
- [Relatius](/guies/relatius): *que* (no preposition), *què* (things + prep.), *qui* (people + prep.), *on*; never *lo que*.

## Sentences

- [Subordinades](/guies/subordinades): *perquè* + ind. (because) / + subj. (so that); future time clauses in subjunctive.
- [Condicionals](/guies/condicionals): *si* + present → future; *si* + imperfet subj. → condicional.
- [Estil indirecte](/guies/estil-indirecte): present → imperfet, past → plusquamperfet, future → condicional, imperative → imperfet subj.
- [Passiva i impersonals](/guies/passiva-impersonals): *es ven / es venen*, *hi ha*, *ser* + participle in writing.
- [Connectors](/guies/connectors): *però / sinó*, *doncs* = consequence only, *per tant*, *tot i que*, *en canvi*.

## Nouns and small words

- [Articles i apostrofació](/guies/articles-apostrofacio): *l'* except *la* + unstressed *i/u*; *al, del, pel*; *en / la* before names.
- [Gènere i nombre](/guies/genere-nombre): *-a → -es* spelling changes; stressed vowel → *-ns*; *-s/-ç/-x → -os*.
- [Demostratius i possessius](/guies/demostratius-possessius): two degrees (*aquest / aquell*); possessives with article.
- [Quantitatius i comparatius](/guies/quantitatius-comparatius): *gaire* (neg./quest.), *gens de* (uncountable), *cap* (countable); *més que, tan … com, tant … com*.
- [Ser i estar](/guies/ser-estar): location → *ser*; state, gerund, duration → *estar*.
- [Per i per a](/guies/per-per-a): cause/means/route → *per*; recipient/purpose → *per a*; before an infinitive → *per*.
- [Hores i dates](/guies/hores-dates): *un quart / dos quarts / tres quarts de* the **next** hour.
- [Numerals](/guies/numerals): hyphens D-U-C; *dos / dues*; ordinals *1r 2n 3r 4t 5è*.
