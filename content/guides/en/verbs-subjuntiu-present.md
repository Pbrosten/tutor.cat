The subjunctive appears in a **subordinate clause** (after *que*) when the main clause expresses will, need, emotion, doubt or purpose — and the two clauses have **different subjects**. With the same subject, use the infinitive:

- Vull **anar** a la platja. (I want → I go: infinitive)
- Vull **que vinguis** a la platja. (I want → you come: subjunctive)

## Forms

The endings swap vowel with the present indicative: -ar verbs take **-i**, everything else takes **-i** too, but from a different stem.

| | cantar | perdre | dormir | servir (incoatiu) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| jo | cant**i** | perd**i** | dorm**i** | serv**eixi** |
| tu | cant**is** | perd**is** | dorm**is** | serv**eixis** |
| ell/ella | cant**i** | perd**i** | dorm**i** | serv**eixi** |
| nosaltres | cant**em** | perd**em** | dorm**im** | serv**im** |
| vosaltres | cant**eu** | perd**eu** | dorm**iu** | serv**iu** |
| ells/elles | cant**in** | perd**in** | dorm**in** | serv**eixin** |

*nosaltres / vosaltres* are identical to the present indicative.

Irregular stems (the 1st person singular of the indicative usually gives the stem: tinc → tingui):

**ser → sigui · estar → estigui · anar → vagi · fer → faci · tenir → tingui · venir → vingui · poder → pugui · voler → vulgui · saber → sàpiga · dir → digui · veure → vegi · haver → hagi · caldre → calgui · beure → begui · viure → visqui · conèixer → conegui · dur → dugui · treure → tregui**

## When to use it (B1)

| trigger | example |
|---|---|
| will / desire: *vull que, voldria que, prefereixo que, espero que* | Espero que **tinguis** sort. |
| need: *cal que, és necessari que, fa falta que* | Cal que **arribeu** puntuals. |
| emotion / judgement: *m'agrada que, em sap greu que, és important que, és normal que* | Em sap greu que no **puguis** venir. |
| doubt / possibility: *dubto que, és possible que, no crec que* | No crec que **plogui**. (but: Crec que **plourà** — indicative) |
| purpose: *perquè* = so that | T'ho explico perquè ho **entenguis**. (but: *perquè* = because → indicative) |
| future time: *quan, fins que, abans que, tan bon punt* | Quan **acabis**, truca'm. |
| negative imperative | No **cantis** tan fort! No **hi aneu**. |

Spelling: -car → -qui (busqui), -gar → -gui (pagui), -çar → -ci (comenci), -jar → -gi (mengi).
