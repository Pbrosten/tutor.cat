Weak pronouns replace a complement so you don't repeat it. At B1 you need the **direct object** (CD), **indirect object** (CI), **en**, **ho** and **hi**.

## Which pronoun?

| replaces | pronoun | example |
|---|---|---|
| CD masc. sing. (*el llibre*) | **el** | Compro el llibre → **El** compro. |
| CD fem. sing. (*la carta*) | **la** | Escric la carta → **La** escric. |
| CD masc. pl. | **els** | **Els** compro. |
| CD fem. pl. | **les** | **Les** escric. |
| CD neuter (*això, allò*, a clause) | **ho** | Saps que ve? — Sí, **ho** sé. |
| CD without article / quantity (*pa, tres pomes*) | **en** | Vols pa? — Sí, **en** vull. Tinc tres germans → **En** tinc tres. |
| CI (*a la Maria, als nens*) | **li** (sing.) / **els** (pl.) | Dono el regal a la Maria → **Li** dono el regal. |
| me / you / us | **em, et, ens, us** | **Et** truco demà. |
| place (*a Girona, al mercat*) | **hi** | Vas al mercat? — Sí, **hi** vaig. |
| origin (*de Girona, d'aquí*) | **en** | Véns de la feina? — Sí, **en** vinc. |

Test for CD vs CI: if the complement has **a** in front and is a person, it is CI → *li / els*. Otherwise it is CD → *el / la / els / les*.

## Where does it go?

**Before** a conjugated verb; **after** (attached) an infinitive, gerund or affirmative imperative. With a periphrasis (*vull comprar*, *estic llegint*) both are correct.

- **El** vull comprar. = Vull comprar**-lo**.
- **L'**estic llegint. = Estic llegint**-lo**.
- Compra**-lo**! · No **el** compris!

## Spelling

| | before consonant | before vowel / h | after consonant | after vowel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| em | **em** dutxo | **m'**aixeco | dutxar**-me** | dutxa**'m** |
| et | **et** veig | **t'**escolto | veure**-te** | mira**'t** |
| es | **es** lleva | **s'**adorm | llevar**-se** | lleva**'t**¹ |
| el | **el** compro | **l'**obro | comprar**-lo** | compra**'l** |
| la | **la** veig | **l'**obro² | veure**-la** | mira**-la** |
| els | **els** veig | **els** obro | veure**-los** | mira**'ls** |
| les | **les** veig | **les** obro | veure**-les** | mira**-les** |
| ho | **ho** sé | **ho** entenc | saber**-ho** | digue**-ho** |
| en | **en** vull | **n'**hi ha | comprar**-ne** | compra**'n** |
| li | **li** dic | **li** escric | dir**-li** | digue**-li** |
| hi | **hi** vaig | **hi** anem | anar**-hi** | vés**-hi** |
| ens | **ens** veiem | **ens** ajuda | veure**'ns**³ | mira**'ns** |
| us | **us** veig | **us** ajudo | veure**-us** | mira**-us** |

¹ imperative of a reflexive is *'t* (lleva't), not *-se*. ² **la** stays **la** before unstressed *i/u*: *la ignora, la uneix*. ³ *-nos* after a consonant: *rentar-nos*.

## Examples

- Has vist la pel·lícula? — Sí, **l'**he vista. (participle may agree with *la/les*)
- Vols cafè? — No, no **en** vull, gràcies.
- Has trucat als teus pares? — Sí, **els** he trucat.
- Dona-**li** les claus. / No **li** donis les claus.
- Coneixes Barcelona? — Sí, **hi** he viscut dos anys.
- Que sap on és? — No **ho** sap.
