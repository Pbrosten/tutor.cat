**Infinitive + -é, -às, -à, -em, -eu, -an.** Verbs in *-re* drop the final *-e*: perdre → perd**ré**.

{{verb cantar futur}}

Irregular stems (the same stems give the conditional):

| infinitive | future | infinitive | future |
|---|---|---|---|
| fer | **faré** | tenir | **tindré** |
| venir | **vindré** | poder | **podré** |
| voler | **voldré** | saber | **sabré** |
| haver | **hauré** | anar | **aniré** |
| valer | **valdré** | caldre | **caldrà** |
| ser | seré | dir | diré |
| veure | veuré | estar | estaré |

Everything else is regular: *seré, estaré, diré, veuré, obriré, dormiré, escriuré, beuré, viuré*.

## Uses

1. Plans and predictions: **Demà plourà. L'any que ve aniré a Itàlia.**
2. Promises: **T'ho enviaré aquesta tarda.**
3. Probability about the present (B1+): **Quina hora deu ser? Seran les deu.** (usually *deure* + infinitive: *deuen ser les deu*)

For an arranged near future the present is enough: **Demà tinc metge.**

Do **not** use *anar a + infinitive* for the future — *vaig a cantar* means "I go (somewhere) to sing", and *vaig cantar* is the past.

After **quan, mentre, fins que, tan bon punt** referring to the future, Catalan uses the **subjunctive**, not the future: *Quan **arribis**, truca'm* (not *quan arribaràs*). *Si* takes the present: *Si **plou**, no sortirem.*
