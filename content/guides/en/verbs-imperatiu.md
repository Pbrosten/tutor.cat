Five persons: **tu, vostè, nosaltres, vosaltres, vostès**. There is no *jo*.

## Affirmative

| person | rule | cantar | perdre | dormir | servir |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tu | = present indicative, 3rd sing. | cant**a** | perd | dorm | serv**eix** |
| vostè | = present subjunctive | cant**i** | perd**i** | dorm**i** | serv**eixi** |
| nosaltres | = present subjunctive | cant**em** | perd**em** | dorm**im** | serv**im** |
| vosaltres | = present indicative, 2nd pl. | cant**eu** | perd**eu** | dorm**iu** | serv**iu** |
| vostès | = present subjunctive | cant**in** | perd**in** | dorm**in** | serv**eixin** |

Irregulars — *tu* and *vosaltres* need learning; *vostè / nosaltres / vostès* are just the subjunctive:

| | tu | vosaltres | vostè |
|---|---|---|---|
| ser | **sigues** | **sigueu** | sigui |
| estar | **estigues** | **estigueu** | estigui |
| tenir | **tingues** (té) | **tingueu** | tingui |
| venir | **vine** | **veniu** | vingui |
| fer | **fes** | **feu** | faci |
| dir | **digues** | **digueu** | digui |
| anar | **vés** | **aneu** | vagi |
| veure | **veges** (mira) | **vegeu** | vegi |

## Negative = no + present subjunctive, all persons

- No **cantis** tan fort. No **canteu** aquí. No **canti**, si us plau.
- No **tinguis** por. No **hi aneu**. No **ho digueu** a ningú.

## Pronouns

Attached after an affirmative imperative (hyphen after a consonant, apostrophe after a vowel), before a negative one:

- Compra**'l**! → No **el** compris!
- Dona**-m'ho**! → No **m'ho** donis!
- Vés**-hi**! → No **hi** vagis!
- Renta**'t** les mans! → No **et** rentis les mans aquí!

Softeners: *si us plau*, *sisplau* (colloquial), or a question with *poder*: **Pots** tancar la porta?
