Three conjugations, four models. The **-ir** group splits in two: *pure* (dormir, sortir, obrir) and *incoatiu* with **-eix-** (servir, and most -ir verbs: llegir, escriure no — that's -re; preferir, decidir, patir, construir…).

| | cant**ar** | perd**re** | dorm**ir** (pur) | serv**ir** (incoatiu) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| jo | cant**o** | perd**o** | dorm**o** | serv**eixo** |
| tu | cant**es** | perd**s** | dorm**s** | serv**eixes** |
| ell/ella | cant**a** | perd | dorm | serv**eix** |
| nosaltres | cant**em** | perd**em** | dorm**im** | serv**im** |
| vosaltres | cant**eu** | perd**eu** | dorm**iu** | serv**iu** |
| ells/elles | cant**en** | perd**en** | dorm**en** | serv**eixen** |

Pure -ir verbs are few: **dormir, sortir (surto), obrir (obro), omplir, cosir, collir (cullo), fugir, tossir, bullir, morir, sentir, mentir**. When in doubt, a verb in -ir is incoatiu.

## Spelling changes (-ar verbs)

-car → -qu-: bus**qu**es · -gar → -gu-: pa**gu**es · -çar → -c-: comen**c**es · -jar → -g-: men**g**es · -guar → -gü-: enai**gü**es.

## Uses

Habits (*cada dia treballo*), general truths, ongoing actions (*ara plou*, or *estic treballant*), and the near future with a time marker: **Demà vaig al metge.** (No *anar a + infinitive* for the future — that's Spanish.)
